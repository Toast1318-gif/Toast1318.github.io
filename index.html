      📝 Quick Notes
      One line per person — a colon, dash, or word like "got"/"had" between the name and the order all work (e.g. "Vijay - 10 wings" or "Vijay got 10 wings"). A line can also just be "tax 9 dollars" or "tip 20 percent" on its own. This text is the master list: every time you tap "Populate Tab," each person's order is rebuilt to exactly match their line (fix "10 wings" to "5" and it becomes 5, not 15), and anyone whose line you remove gets removed from the tab too. "Undo this import" reverts the whole tab to how it was right before you tapped Populate.
      🧾 Receipt Check
      Take a photo (or upload) of the actual bar receipt. This is read right on your device — nothing is uploaded anywhere — and checked against everything currently on the tab. Since a receipt only shows totals per item for the whole table (not who ordered what), this is a whole-tab sanity check — "does everything add up" — not a per-person match. You can also pull the tax & tip straight from the receipt instead of typing them in. OCR isn't perfect, especially on blurry or angled photos, so review and fix the recognized text below before comparing.
